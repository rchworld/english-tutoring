## Why some HR certificates carry more weight than others

A certificate can prove more than the fact that you finished a course. So before you choose, be clear about what you want the certificate to do for you.

Many introductory HR courses on general learning platforms, including university-led specializations for people managers, award a certificate once you complete the lessons and quizzes. That certificate confirms you covered the basics, which is useful if you’re new to HR or starting to manage people, though it tells an employer little about whether you can apply those skills in the role you’re in now.

If you want a certificate that shows what you can do on the job, look for a program that asks you to apply your skills before you earn it. AIHR Certificate Programs work this way. Each one combines video lessons, practical exercises, and case studies, and ends with a two-part capstone project. In the capstone assignment, you apply what you’ve learned to a realistic scenario and review your own work. In the capstone exam, you need a score of at least 70% to earn the certificate.

When you pass, you get a digital certificate to share and verify on LinkedIn. Every AIHR Certificate Program is recognized for SHRM PDCs toward SHRM-CP or SHRM-SCP recertification. AIHR is also an HRCI-approved provider, and HRPA, CPHR, and many other HR professional associations recognize its learning. So the certificate shows you built the skill, and it counts toward a credential you already hold.

## What to check before you enroll in an online HR course

A few minutes of research upfront can save you weeks on a certificate that doesn’t fit your goal. Before you pay, check these five things:

- ✔ Who issues it: Look for a dedicated HR training provider, an HR association, or a university. Check that the certificate names the issuer, the skills covered, and the completion date. A named program is more likely to catch a recruiter’s or hiring manager’s eye on your résumé or LinkedIn profile.

- ✔ Recertification value: If you already hold a credential, confirm the credit type and number of credits. AIHR, for example, is recognized by SHRM to offer PDCs for SHRM-CP or SHRM-SCP recertification activities.

- ✔ Skill-building or exam prep: Decide whether you need to apply a specific HR capability now, like building a compensation structure or analyzing people data , or prepare for a certification exam.

- ✔ Format and workload: Check whether it’s self-paced or cohort-based, plus the weekly hours, deadlines, assessments, and access period.

- ✔ Full cost: See whether the certificate is included or sold as an add-on. Look for exam fees, subscription renewals, and a money-back guarantee or free preview.

## FAQ

Can you get an HR certificate online with no experience? Yes. Many online HR courses and certificate programs have no eligibility criteria and no prerequisites. AIHR’s HR Coordinator Certificate Program, for instance, is designed for people new to HR or aiming for their first HR job. Formal certifications are different: the PHR, for example, requires professional HR experience.

Do online HR courses count toward SHRM recertification? Some do. AIHR is recognized by SHRM to offer PDCs for SHRM-CP or SHRM-SCP recertification activities, and select LinkedIn Learning courses also qualify. Check the exact credit count for each course before you enroll. You can find the credits AIHR awards in detail on the accredited HR training page.

What’s the fastest online HR certificate to get? HR courses on platforms like AIHR usually take two to 10 hours and award a certificate of completion. A certificate program that tests applied skills takes longer, usually 25 to 45 hours, but it gives you more to show an employer or a manager.
