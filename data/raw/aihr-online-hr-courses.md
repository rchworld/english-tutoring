## Why do an online HR course?

There are many benefits to completing online HR courses:

- Flexible learning: The online courses featured in this article allow learning from anywhere in the world. As they’re largely self-paced, they allow you to plan your learning journey around your current job or commitments.

- Cost-effectiveness: Many online HR courses cost a fraction of the cost of a formal degree. These courses are shorter and let you specialize in the HR areas that interest you.

- Long-term access : Many of these online HR courses offer long-term or even lifetime access to the content, so you can revisit specific modules whenever you need a refresher.

- Building self-discipline: Completing an online, self-paced course requires motivation and discipline, which are great qualities to develop and carry with you throughout your career.

- Getting financing from your employer: If you’re already employed as an HR professional, there’s a strong chance you can get your organization to pay for you to complete an online HR course or certification. Employers want to invest in you because, in the long run, this is an investment in the business.

## How to choose the right online HR training: 5 factors

Two courses can look identical on paper and still serve very different goals. The right Human Resources training for you depends on where you want your career to go, how you learn best, and what your schedule and budget allow. Work through the five factors below before you enroll.

### 1. Start with your career direction

Before comparing providers, determine the role you’re working toward. AIHR’s HR Career Map shows the skills and typical progression for each HR role, making it easier to pick training that moves you forward. A course that excites you but doesn’t build the skills your target role requires will cost you time you could spend on one that does.

### 2. Decide between practical skills and a formal credential

Some HR training courses teach hands-on skills you can apply the same week, while others prepare you for a formal certificate that carries weight on your résumé. If your job market values credentials, prioritize programs that provide a recognized certificate. If you need to close a specific skills gap fast, a short practical course may serve you better. The two paths complement each other, and many HR professionals combine both over time.

### 3. Match the format to your schedule

HR training online ranges from 90-minute modules to programs that run for several months. Check the weekly time commitment against your calendar honestly. Self-paced formats give you flexibility, while cohort-based or facilitated courses add structure and peer discussion. Pick the format you’ll actually finish, not the one that looks most impressive.

### 4. Set your budget and check for sponsorship

Options on this list range from free introductory courses to premium certificate programs. Before ruling anything out based on price, ask whether your employer will sponsor your Human Resources training online. Many organizations fund professional development because it pays back into the business, and a short business case is often all it takes to get your course expensed.

### 5. Confirm recertification credits

If you already hold a SHRM or HRCI credential, check whether a course or certificate program carries recertification credits before you decide to enroll. Training that counts toward your renewal lets you build new skills and maintain your credential in one move instead of paying for each separately.
